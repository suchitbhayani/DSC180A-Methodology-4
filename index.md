**1. What is the most interesting topic covered in your domain this quarter?**
The interesting topic I explored this quarter was using ontologies in AI for drug discovery. Ontologies, like Gene Ontology and Protein Ontology, organize biological information about genes, proteins, and their functions. AI can use this structured knowledge to better understand how drugs interact with multiple proteins and predict possible side effects.
